TF2Speech v{version} - Voices
==========================

Every voice below shows up in all of TF2Speech's voice lists (default voice, /v voice commands, per-player
voices, the DJ voice) as soon as it's available. Too many? Voice Commands tab -> select voices in the list of
available voices -> "Hide / Unhide Selected". Hidden voices are left out of every list and never given out at
random; select them and press it again to bring them back.


WORKS OUT OF THE BOX
====================

DECtalk (built in)
------------------
Perfect Paul, Harry, Frank, Betty, Wendy, Dennis, Kit, Ursula and Rita - the Moonbase Alpha voices - with
DECtalk's phoneme and singing commands ([:np], [dah<600,20>]...). Nothing to install. DECtalk commands sent with
any other voice are spoken with DECtalk automatically.

Windows voices (SAPI 5)
-----------------------
The voices that come with Windows (Microsoft David, Zira... depending on your Windows language).

Any other SAPI 5 voice installed on the PC for 64-bit programs shows up too, e.g. voices you bought from
IVONA, Cepstral, CereProc or NeoSpeech. Voices that were only installed for 32-bit programs don't show up,
because TF2Speech is a 64-bit program.


FREE EXTRAS - ONE CLICK IN THE VOICES TAB
=========================================

Windows' extra voices (no download)
-----------------------------------
Windows 10/11 can add more voices and languages: Windows Settings -> Time & language -> Speech -> Add voices.
Windows keeps those apart from what normal programs can use, so after adding some, open TF2Speech's Voices tab
and press "Make them usable here" (Windows asks for admin permission once), then restart TF2Speech.
"Undo" removes them again. Many PCs already have one or two of these (e.g. Microsoft Mark).

Piper voices (free download)
----------------------------
Natural-sounding neural voices in dozens of languages. They run on your PC - nothing you type is sent online.
Voices tab -> "Download Piper (22 MB)" the first time -> pick a voice -> "Download" (20-130 MB each) -> "Test".
They show up as  [Piper] <voice>,  e.g. [Piper] en_US-ryan-medium. Downloads come from the official Piper
voice list and are checked against its checksums. Each voice has its own licence (see its MODEL_CARD).


IF YOU HAVE THEM INSTALLED - NOT INCLUDED, AND TF2SPEECH CAN'T DOWNLOAD THEM
============================================================================

Classic SAPI 4 voices: Microsoft Sam 4.0, TruVoice (BonziBuddy) and other 90s voices
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What you need installed (works on 64-bit Windows 10/11):
  - The Microsoft Speech API 4.0 runtime (spchapi.exe)
  - The voices, for example:
      "Microsoft Text-to-Speech Engine 4.0 (English)"
          Sam, Mike and Mary, Mike / Mary in Hall, in Space, in Stadium and for Telephone,
          RoboSoft One - Six, Male / Female Whisper
      "Lernout & Hauspie TruVoice American English TTS Engine" (tv_enua.exe)
          Adult Male #1 - #8, Adult Female #1 - #2
These were free Microsoft Agent downloads; Microsoft no longer hosts them, and TF2Speech can't include them.
Install them as administrator, then restart TF2Speech.

Voices tab -> "Classic SAPI 4 voices" shows how many were found, with a Test button and a volume slider.
They show up as  [SAPI4] <voice>,  e.g. [SAPI4] Sam or [SAPI4] Adult Male #1 (TruVoice), plus a ready-made

    [SAPI4] Bonzi  =  TruVoice Adult Male #2 at BonziBuddy's pitch (140) and speed (157)

which appears whenever TruVoice is installed. SAPI 4 voices are 32-bit only, so TF2Speech uses a small helper
of its own (sapi4_tts.exe, built into the exe; source on GitHub, MIT). SAPI 4 codes typed in chat (like
\Pau=9000\) are read out as text, not obeyed. Without SAPI 4, the Voices tab just says it isn't installed.

TextAloud voices, e.g. Acapela "Will From Afar"
-----------------------------------------------
Some voices sold by NextUp for TextAloud only work inside TextAloud.
What you need: TextAloud 3 (tested with 3.0.119), registered with your own licence, plus the voice installed
for it. A "TextAloud" tab then appears in TF2Speech, and its voices show up as  [TextAloud] <voice>.

TF2Speech runs TextAloud on a separate, hidden Windows desktop, so its window and error boxes never pop up over
your game. If TextAloud crashes or freezes it's restarted and the line is tried again; if it keeps failing, its
voices use your default voice for a few minutes. If you have TextAloud open yourself, TF2Speech uses that copy
and leaves it open. Without TextAloud, the tab is hidden.


ALL VOICES
==========
!stop, mutes, blocks, the output device and the DJ's music ducking work the same with every kind of voice. If a
[Piper], [SAPI4] or [TextAloud] voice someone picked isn't available (uninstalled, removed, not working), that
line is spoken in your default voice instead.
